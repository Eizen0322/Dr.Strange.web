ABOUT DOCTOR STRANGE

The Man Behind the Magic

Stephen Strange

Stephen Strange was a brilliant neurosurgeon whose life changed after a devastating accident. Searching for a way to heal himself, he discovered Kamar-Taj and learned the ancient Mystic Arts. He eventually became the Sorcerer Supreme and protector of Earth against supernatural threats.
